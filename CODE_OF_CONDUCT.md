# Community conduct / 社区行为准则

CoBot welcomes contributions in English and Chinese from people with
different backgrounds and levels of experience. Be respectful, discuss
ideas and evidence, and give actionable feedback. Disagreement is welcome;
harassment, threats, discrimination, personal attacks, spam and publishing
someone else's private information are not.

The maintainer may moderate discussions, remove harmful content, close
disruptive threads or restrict participation. To raise a conduct concern,
email [kunghimxu@gmail.com](mailto:kunghimxu@gmail.com) with the subject
`CoBot conduct report`. Include relevant links and context privately;
do not repost private or harmful material publicly. Reports are considered
in context; do not use this policy to suppress respectful technical disagreement.

CoBot 欢迎不同背景和经验水平的贡献者使用中文或英文参与。请尊重他人，围绕事实
和方案讨论，提出可操作的反馈。欢迎不同意见，不接受骚扰、威胁、歧视、人身攻击、
垃圾信息或公开他人隐私。

维护者可管理讨论、移除有害内容、关闭扰乱讨论的帖子或限制参与。行为问题请邮件联系
[kunghimxu@gmail.com](mailto:kunghimxu@gmail.com)，主题写 `CoBot conduct report`，
私下提供相关链接和背景，不要在公开帖子重复传播私人或有害资料。维护者会结合
上下文处理；本准则不应用于压制有礼的技术分歧。
